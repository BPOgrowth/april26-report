⚠ Attribution Gap — Investigate

Google "unknown" (7.02% conv) and "unattributed paid" (8.06% conv) together drove $102K in revenue with extremely high conversion rates. These are likely mis-tagged UTMs or direct referrals from Shopping campaigns not properly attributed. Audit UTM parameters immediately.

⚠ AOV Paid vs Organic Gap

Google Paid AOV is $237 vs Organic $333 — a $96 per-order gap. Paid campaigns are attracting lower-value buyers, or Shopping campaigns (which dominate paid) bias toward cheaper items. Review campaign structure and product-price targeting.

🔥 ChatGPT = Highest-Intent Traffic

ChatGPT sends 709 combined sessions converting at 6.5–8.2% — 3.4–4.3x better than Google organic (1.89%). Revenue: $9,306/month with zero investment. BuyParts has 0% AI visibility. Scaling this is the highest-ROI opportunity in the report.

✅ Email = 14.11% Conv Rate

Shopify Email drives a 14.11% conversion rate from just 163 sessions — the highest of any paid channel. Only 23 orders in April. This channel is massively underutilized. Every email campaign beats every paid ad in conversion efficiency.

⛔ Demand Gen = Burning Money

Demand Gen Campaign spent $2,592 to generate just $3,090 in conversion value — 1.19x ROAS. With a target ROAS of 8–10x, this campaign is losing ~$1,400/month. Pause immediately and reallocate to PMAX Truck OEM ($150+) at 10.51x ROAS.

⛔ CATCH ALL Bleeders Inefficiency

CATCH ALL Bleeders has 16,403 clicks (most of any campaign) at 3.43x ROAS — well below the account average of ~8x. It's consuming budget on low-quality products or irrelevant queries. Reduce budget 50% and add negative keywords.

✅ Mobile = 63% of Conversions

Mobile generated 961 of 1,516 total conversions (63.4%) while spending 53% of budget. Mobile is the more efficient device. Consider increasing mobile bid adjustments +15–20% and testing mobile-specific landing pages.

✅ AI Search Campaign at 9.61x ROAS

The AI Search Campaign is already running and performing at 9.61x ROAS — above average. This validates the AEO/GEO thesis. Double its budget from $2,254 to $4,500/month and add AI-specific ad copy variants targeting ChatGPT-style queries.
